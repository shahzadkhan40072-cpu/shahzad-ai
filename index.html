Independent project

SHAHZAD AI is an independent personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to Google or YouTube. YouTube features depend on Google's APIs and the permissions you grant.

The current desktop monitor is a read-only companion: it does not upload videos, reply to comments, edit channel content, or guarantee real-time alerts. Your computer and internet connection must remain active for monitoring.
