Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how the current SHAHZAD AI desktop YouTube monitoring application handles information when you choose to connect a Google/YouTube account.

1. Information accessed

With your authorization, the app accesses YouTube information permitted by the Google OAuth permissions shown during sign-in. The current version is intended for read-only monitoring and may retrieve channel statistics, recent video details/statistics and comments available through the authorized YouTube Data API scope.

2. How information is used

Information is used to display your channel overview and detect selected activity changes, such as new uploads, new comments and configured view-count increases, so the app can show alerts.

3. Local storage and security

The current desktop version stores its OAuth authorization token and monitoring state locally on the computer where you run it. SHAHZAD AI does not currently operate a central server that collects or stores your YouTube account data. Keep your computer account secure and do not share application token files or OAuth credentials.

4. Optional Telegram notifications

If you configure Telegram notifications, alert text generated by the app is sent to the Telegram bot/chat details you provide. Telegram then handles that information under its own terms and privacy practices. This feature is optional.

5. Sharing and selling

The current app does not sell your personal information. It does not send YouTube account data to advertising networks. Data is shared with Google/YouTube as necessary to use their APIs and, only if enabled by you, with Telegram for notifications.

6. Data retention and deletion

Monitoring state and OAuth token files remain on your computer until you remove them. You can stop access by revoking SHAHZAD AI's Google account access in your Google Account security settings, and you can delete the app's local token/state files from your computer. Deleting local files does not itself revoke Google's authorization.

7. Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs by SHAHZAD AI will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can review Google's policy at Google API Services User Data Policy.

8. Children's privacy

This project is not designed to knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are made.
